Multi-party swap planner

Add every trader (including you). Each person lists offers (spares they can ship) and needs. The planner routes each sticker from someone who has a spare to someone else who still needs that code, never to the same person. It maximizes total stickers moved across the whole group, respects each person's optional Max receive cap, and uses Priority (lower = better) only to break ties. Lists support ALG1(2) and empty CSV gaps like the other tools.
