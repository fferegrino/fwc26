Hub swap planner

Everything is centered on you: you list your offers and needs, then each other swapper lists theirs. The planner only moves stickers from you to them (your offers ∩ their needs) and from them to you (their offers ∩ your needs). It does not plan trades between two other people with no involvement from you. It maximizes how many stickers you send and how many you receive (two independent flows), with optional caps and the same CSV rules as the other tools.
