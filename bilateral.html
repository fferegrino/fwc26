Bilateral swap planner

Enter what each side can trade (spares / dupes) and what each side still needs. The planner finds how many stickers can move in each direction where your offer overlaps their need and vice versa. Balanced 1:1 caps both directions at the same count so neither side gives more stickers than they get (equal swap size). Independent max shows the maximum each direction could move without linking counts—useful to see upside before you negotiate a bundle. Lists support repeats and ALG1(2) like the other tools.

How to count the trade

Each sticker counts as one unit. This planner does not score “rare vs common”; it maximizes how many useful trades happen, then breaks ties with your priority numbers.
